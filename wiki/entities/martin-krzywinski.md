# Martin Krzywinski

Staff scientist at Canada's Michael Smith Genome Sciences Center / BC Cancer Research Center, Vancouver. Creator of [[hive-plot|hive plots]] and Circos (circular genome visualization tool).

## Contributions to Hive Plots

- Introduced hive plots in [[krzywinski-2012|Krzywinski et al. 2012]]
- Co-authored [[perez-2021-hype|HyPE]] (Hive Panel Explorer, 2021)
- Introduced [[differential-hive-plot|differential hive plots]] in [[krzywinski-2017-differential|2017]]
- Maintains the reference website at hiveplot.com
- Co-authored the original Perl reference implementation and jhive (Java)

## Key Insight

Argued that [[force-directed-layout|force-directed layouts]] are fundamentally flawed for network analysis because they lack reproducibility and perceptual uniformity. Proposed hive plots as a "rational" alternative where node positions derive from meaningful structural properties.
