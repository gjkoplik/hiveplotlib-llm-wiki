## Goal

After the NetworkX metric-expansion sprint, `examples/computing_graph_metrics.ipynb` mixes `HivePlot` and `HivePlotMatrix` content across three datasets (Karate Club, Les Misérables, the synthetic toy). This plan splits that content cleanly: `computing_graph_metrics.ipynb` becomes a focused `HivePlot`-only **gallery** (Karate for the network demos, the synthetic toy for pure mechanics), and the `HivePlotMatrix` / Les Misérables material that left the gallery is re-homed into a new **tutorial** (`finding_a_partition.ipynb`, the "partition discovery" arc) that handed off to the existing `hive_plots_more_than_three_groups.ipynb` menu of >3-group options. When this ships, a user reaching for "how do I compute a metric on a hive plot" lands on a coherent gallery. No user-facing API changes; this is a docs-only restructure.
