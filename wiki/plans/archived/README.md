# Archived Plans

Shipped plans, kept for history. A plan moves here once its work has fully shipped, typically right after it promotes to an ADR (see `../../adr/`), or after shipping for plans that won't become ADRs.

Active and unstarted plans live one level up in [[README|plans/]]. Keeping shipped plans out of that directory means browsing `plans/` shows only what's still in flight.

Plans are moved here by the human on confirmation, not by agents. Research Liaison proposes archiving (at ADR promotion, or in its post-task pass for non-ADR plans) but never moves files itself.
