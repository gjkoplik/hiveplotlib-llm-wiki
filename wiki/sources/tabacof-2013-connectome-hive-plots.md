# Tabacof, Busbice & Larson 2013 — Beyond the Connectome Hairball (C. elegans Hive Plots)

**Citation:** Tabacof, A., Busbice, T. and Larson, S.D. (OpenWorm), 2013. Beyond the connectome hairball: Rational visualizations and analysis of the C. elegans connectome as a network graph using hive plots. *Frontiers in Neuroinformatics*, Neuroinformatics 2013 conference abstract. DOI: 10.3389/conf.fninf.2013.09.00032.

## Summary

The first published [[hive-plot|hive plot]] of the *C. elegans* connectome. Places neurons on three axes by functional class (sensory / interneuron / motor) and draws synaptic connectivity as curves, arguing that a rational, quantitative layout reads where the force-directed "hairball" does not. The abstract self-claims, verbatim, "to the best of our knowledge this is the first application of the hive plot visualization technique to any connectome data set."

The "beyond the hairball" framing (a hive plot as the organized alternative to a force-directed tangle) is exactly the rhetorical move a connectome hive-plot demonstration leans on.
